Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used to operate TreasureTales.

Information we collect

We may collect basic account information, location data while the app is in use, adventure progress and content you choose to upload.

How we use information

We use information to provide account access, show nearby adventures, save progress, operate routes and improve the app.

Camera and augmented reality

The camera provides the live AR experience. Camera imagery is not stored by us during normal use.

Location

Location helps show nearby adventures, recognize arrival at stops and activate stages. You can disable permission in device settings.

Sharing and retention

The app may use system services and sign-in providers. We do not sell personal information and retain it only as long as needed.

Children and safety

Children should use the app with adult supervision and remain aware of traffic and their surroundings.

Your choices

You can manage permissions in device settings and request information, correction or deletion by emailing netanelvision@gmail.com.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the service changes.
